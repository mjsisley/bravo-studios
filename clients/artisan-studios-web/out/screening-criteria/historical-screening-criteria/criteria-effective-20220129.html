Artisan Studios Screening Criteria

Before applying to lease a studio we require:

  • No application will be accepted until all potential renters in your party have seen and completed a walkthrough or virtual tour of the property with a representative of Artisan Studios
  • Applicants must show up on time for showing appointment or reschedule with a timely call (failure to do so will result in denial of the application).
  • Positive picture ID (State or Government) must be presented at showing.
  • Once you have reviewed the unit and want to move forward with an application you may request an application in person or use the Zillow application app to submit an application per our General guidelines and Criteria
  • General Guidelines: An application must be completed for every occupant 18 years and older A $45 non-refundable application fee shall be paid for each applicant 18 years and older ($29 may be waived if using Zillow application system) We do not accept comprehensive reusable tenant screening reports, except from Zillow. In the event of denial or other adverse action you have the right to obtain a free copy of the consumer report, and to dispute the accuracy of the information appearing in the consumer report.we use consumer reports from:
    • Experian
      • PO Box 9600, Allen, TX 75013.
      • Phone: (800) 397-3742
      • Website: www.experian.com
  • Applicant(s) must be able to pay the required deposits(s) and rent at the time of approval and signing of lease agreement.
  • Applications must be fully completed with no false or material misstatements or material omissions for all potential occupants of a studio to be considered complete. Incomplete applications will be denied. Completed Application(s) for a studio are processed in the order received “first in time”
  • You may request additional time to complete the application if you have a legitimate request for a reasonable accommodation in which case if granted your place in line for processing “first in time” for a completed application will be the request date if submitted by the extra time allowed.
  • Completed applications may be submitted to the Zillow system, by email to Artisan Studios, or in person to an Artisan Studios representative. The time of receipt for “first in time” will be the date & time stamp of your email, zillows email to us, or the date and time noted by the Artisan representative on your in person submission, of a completed application package by each method.
  • Positive picture ID (State or Government) must be submitted with application

Income & Employment Requirements

  • Adequate gross income to rent ratio of 2.75 to 1 (rent should not exceed 36% of your income).

  • No excessive debt which may impact applicant(s) ability to pay rent. Debt payments to income ratio shall not exceed 25% of the applicants' income.

  • If income and employment requirements are not met, an approved co-signer may be added to the lease in place of this requirement (see approved co-signer qualificaitons section of criteria).

  • Employment Verification: There must be proof of verifiable employment and/or verifiable source of income, as follows:

    • W-2 Employees:

      • Provide the 2 most recent pay stubs (Employer /Employee /Date of pay period) Or Fully executed job offer letter/contract, with last W-2.
      • Employer Reference contact and phone number.

    • Self Employed:

      • 3 months of bank statements and signed tax returns for the last 2 years to show
        predictable income (W-2, 1099, first 3 pages on form 1040).

    • Alternative Source of Income:

      • Other verifiable sources of Income which include but are not limited to housing or rental assistance programs, social security, veterans benefits, retirement or other supplemental income and other programs administered by federal, state, local governments or non-profit organizations. The alternative sources of income shall be combined into an annual amount then divided by 12 for monthly income.
      • Section 8 applicants are required to submit the same criteria as stated above, with the exception that the applicant only needs to meet income requirements for their portion of the rent.

Public Records & Rental History

Each applicant must demonstrate a pattern of meeting their rental or mortgage obligations, leaving properties in good condition, and not having a history of documented legal or lease violations. To prove:

  • Provide references from previous housing providers(s) for a period of the past 3 years. If there is no rental history an approved co-signer (see approved co-signer qualificaitons section of criteria) may be added to the lease in place of this requirement.
  • No documented comply or vacate notices or late payments
  • No history of evictions, except:
    • Eviction history will not be considered for the time period of the City of Seattle State of Emergency and six months following unless the eviction is due to actions by the tenant constituting an imminent threat to the health and safety of neighbors, the landlord, or the tenant or landlord's household members.

Credit History

  • A minimum credit score of 700 is required.
  • If your credit score is between 650 and 699 you may add an approved co-signer (see approved co-signer qualificaitons section of criteria).

Approved Co-Signer Qualifications

Application of co-signer to be submitted with application for verification and approval.

The co-signer will be Approved if ALL of the qualifications below are met:

  • 1 year of valid and verifiable rental or mortgage history with no late payments or evidence of home ownership free of debt.
  • Minimum Experian credit score of 750 with all credit accounts in good standing.
  • Employment: Employment verification critera is to be the same as for a prospective tenant. Included above as a subsection of the Income & Employment Section.
  • Income: 4.5 times the rental amount of the unit with verifiable garnishable income.

Policies Summary

  • No parking on site available, RPZ parking zone 6 parking available through the City of Seattle.
  • Pet Policy: No dogs or other animals allowed except for licensed service animals. Cats allowed with $200 pet damage deposit and pet addendum.
  • No smoking allowed in the building.
  • Zero tolerance on drug & alcohol related activity, harassment and noise disturbances.
  • Utility Fees:
    • Garbage: Prorated per full occupancy, roughly $20/month.
    • Water and hot water heating are billed based on submetering.
    • Sewer is based upon pro-ration of base charge and water consumption.
    • Electric: An account with Seattle City Light will be in your name for the unit.
    • High speed internet available via Lumen (CenturyLink) and can be set up immediately with directions in unit.

Notes:

  • Artisan Studios may change criteria over time. The criteria in effect below at the time of your applications’ submittal shall be used in determining your eligibility and will be evaluated in the order received per Seattle law. You are encouraged to apply even if you do not meet the criteria. While you may not be offered tenancy on your application, we will notify you if our posted application criteria changes and invite you to reapply with a waiver of the application fee. All historical screening criteria is available here.
  • SMC 14.09 Fair Chance Ordinance prohibits criminal history screening. Prospective tenants are encouraged to check the sexual offender registry regularly and take precautions they deem necessary. Any criminal background check provided as part of an application is unsolicited and will not be reviewed or considered as part of the application eligibility, as required by Seattle Ordinance.
